Process

How it works

Four steps, no surprises. You always know what is happening and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Send the brief

    Use the form on the portfolio, or email me directly. A rough idea is enough — a link, a document, or three sentences about what you need.

  2. 02

    Scope and quote

    Within two working days you get a written scope: what is included, what is not, the timeline, and a fixed price for that scope.

  3. 03

    Design and build

    I design in the browser and in Figma as needed, and send a working link you can open every week. You see progress as it is built, not a big reveal at the end.

  4. 04

    Launch and hand-off

    Deploy, connect the domain, and walk you through editing it. The code and accounts are yours, and I fix anything I broke for 30 days after launch.

Terms

Payments, plainly

The thing most people want to ask but do not. Here it is without a call.

50% to start, 50% at launch

Half secures the slot in the schedule and starts the work. The second half is due on launch, after you have seen the build running. Larger builds are split into three milestone payments tied to those milestones, not to the calendar.

Bank transfer or card, in GBP or NGN

Invoices are issued before each payment with the scope and the amount on them. International clients pay by card or transfer; Nigerian clients by transfer. No hidden fees are added to the price that was quoted.

Questions

The usual ones

If yours is not here, ask it in the form — I answer every message.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted per scope. A marketing site and a multi-role dashboard are different jobs, and a fixed price for a defined scope is more honest than an hourly rate that grows. As a shape rather than a promise: a five-page site is a four-figure sum in GBP, a dashboard with accounts and live data is five figures. Send the brief and you get a real number in two working days.

How long does it take?

A landing page or small site is usually a week or two. A web app or dashboard runs four to eight weeks depending on how many screens and integrations it needs. The quote includes the timeline, and I tell you early if it slips.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes — most of my work is remote. I overlap with West Africa (WAT) and keep flexible hours for calls with Europe and North America, and everything else runs asynchronously in writing.

What do you need from me?

Three things: the content or the raw material for it, access to the accounts the site will live in, and one person who can make decisions. Everything else — structure, design, build, deployment — is my job.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of fixes for anything I broke, a walkthrough of how to edit the site, and the code in a repository you own. If you want ongoing changes, that is the monthly option above.

Will I own the code?

Yes. On final payment the code, the design files and the accounts are handed over and are yours to keep, change or give to another developer.
