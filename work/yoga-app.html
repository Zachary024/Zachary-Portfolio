Yoga App

Daily flows, class schedules and progress tracking in one calm interface.

Client
Independent studio
Role
Product design and front-end
Timeline
5 weeks

The brief

Students were booking classes on Instagram DMs and following sessions from screenshots saved in their camera roll. Nothing carried over from one week to the next.

The constraint

It had to feel quiet. Most fitness apps shout; the brief was the opposite, and every screen was judged against whether it lowered the heart rate.

What I built

  • A daily flow screen with one primary action and no streaks or badges.
  • A weekly schedule that shows the next class without opening a second screen.
  • Progress kept to three numbers, all of them about consistency rather than performance.
  • A component and type scale that keeps the interface consistent as classes are added.
