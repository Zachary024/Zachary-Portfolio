Interior Studio

An editorial portfolio that lets large photography carry the studio's work.

Client
Studio Bellevue
Role
Design and build
Timeline
4 weeks

The brief

A portfolio with beautiful photographs and a gallery that made them look small. Every project page had the same eleven-shot carousel regardless of the room.

The constraint

The photographs were shot at different times by different people, in four aspect ratios. The layout had to survive all of them without cropping the architecture.

What I built

  • A project template that adapts its grid to the photographs it is given.
  • Full-bleed images with captions that stay out of the frame.
  • A studio page that explains process in four steps, for clients who are new to interiors.
  • Lazy loading and responsive sizes for galleries of twenty-plus images.
